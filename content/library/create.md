<!-- The CREATE activities, in the order an assessment pages through them. -->

## Activity: Explore Solution Concepts
id: explore-solution-concepts
owner: Design / UX

Develop and compare alternative ways of addressing a validated problem before selecting an approach for implementation.

**Try this.** Generate three materially different approaches to the same validated problem before choosing one.

## Activity: Create and Test Prototypes
id: create-and-test-prototypes
owner: Design / UX

Develop representations or simulations of potential solutions to explore concepts and test assumptions before production development.

**Try this.** Build the cheapest representation that can test the riskiest solution assumption with representative users.

## Activity: Interaction Design
id: interaction-design
owner: Design / UX

Design workflows, interactions, information architecture, and interface behaviors that shape the user's experience of the solution.

**Try this.** Trace one important user workflow and identify unnecessary decisions, steps, or points of confusion.

## Activity: Usability Testing
id: usability-testing
owner: Design / UX

Evaluate proposed or implemented solutions with representative users to understand usability, comprehension, and workflow effectiveness.

**Try this.** Observe several representative users attempting an important task without coaching them through it.

## Activity: Partner on Solution Delivery
id: partner-on-solution-delivery
owner: Product Management

Collaborate across product, design, engineering, and other disciplines as an approved solution is developed and refined.

**Try this.** Hold a Product Quad discussion on one problem and make the tradeoffs and decision ownership explicit.

## Activity: Develop Production Software
id: develop-production-software
owner: Engineering

Design, implement, test, review, and maintain software and technical components that become part of the production product.

**Try this.** Use this activity to clarify the engineering boundary: identify where product context ends and engineering implementation ownership begins.

## Activity: Data & Instrumentation Strategy
id: data-instrumentation-strategy
owner: Data / Analytics

Define the data and instrumentation needed to understand product behavior, measure outcomes, and support future product decisions.

**Try this.** For one desired outcome, identify the events and data needed to know whether user behavior actually changed.

## Activity: Release Brief
id: release-brief
owner: Product Management

Define the goals, scope, tradeoffs, dependencies, and intended outcomes of an upcoming release.

**Try this.** Write a short release brief covering goal, scope, tradeoffs, dependencies, target users, and expected outcomes.

## Activity: Manage Project Schedules
id: manage-project-schedules
owner: Project Manager / Program Manager

Coordinate development schedules, dependencies, milestones, and delivery activities across participating teams.

**Try this.** Identify which delivery coordination work belongs to project/program management versus product decision-making.
