<!-- The COMMIT activities, in the order an assessment pages through them. -->

## Activity: Set Strategic Direction
id: set-strategic-direction
owner: Product Management

Establish the product's strategic priorities and guide investment decisions around the outcomes and opportunities that matter most.

**Try this.** List the three product outcomes that matter most this year and identify work that does not support them.

## Activity: Develop Product Vision
id: develop-product-vision
owner: Product Management

Articulate the longer-term direction and aspiration for the product to provide context for decisions and investments.

**Try this.** Write a short description of the future state your product is intended to create, without listing features or dates.

## Activity: Business Plan
id: business-plan
owner: Product Management

Describe the business opportunity, assumptions, investment requirements, financial model, risks, and expected outcomes for an initiative.

**Try this.** For one initiative, document the business opportunity, assumptions, investment, risks, and expected outcomes rather than a feature justification.

## Activity: Size Market Opportunity
id: size-market-opportunity
owner: Product Marketing

Estimate the size and characteristics of the addressable opportunity to inform investment decisions.

**Try this.** Estimate an opportunity from target personas/problems and realistic reach, then sanity-check it against a market proxy.

## Activity: Explore Business Scenarios
id: explore-business-scenarios
owner: Product Management

Evaluate alternative assumptions and scenarios involving adoption, pricing, revenue, cost, retention, market size, or other factors that could affect the business case.

**Try this.** Change three major assumptions in a business case and see which assumptions materially change the investment decision.

## Activity: Challenge Product Assumptions
id: challenge-product-assumptions
owner: Product Management

Examine important assumptions, dependencies, risks, contradictions, and conditions that must be true for an initiative to succeed.

**Try this.** Ask what must be true for one current initiative to succeed and identify the least-supported assumption.

## Activity: Build, Buy, or Partner
id: build-buy-or-partner
owner: Product Management

Evaluate alternative ways to acquire capabilities needed for the offering, including internal development, acquisition, licensing, and partnerships.

**Try this.** For one needed capability, compare internal development, purchase, and partnership using time, strategic advantage, cost, and risk.

## Activity: Product Roadmap
id: product-roadmap
owner: Product Management

Describe the intended path toward product outcomes, including sequencing, dependencies, learning, and major investments.

**Try this.** Remove dates from a roadmap and restate the sequence as outcomes, learning, dependencies, and strategic investments.

## Activity: Pricing and Packaging
id: pricing-and-packaging
owner: Product Marketing

Establish how the offering will be packaged and priced for its target buyers and markets.

**Try this.** Choose one offer and write down the value metric, buyer, alternatives, packaging logic, and assumptions behind the current price.

## Activity: Develop Success Metrics
id: develop-success-metrics
owner: Product Management

Define the business and customer outcomes that indicate whether an initiative is succeeding and how they will be measured.

**Try this.** For one initiative, define the customer behavior and business result expected to change before choosing the metric.

## Activity: Competitive Strategy
id: competitive-strategy
owner: Product Management

Determine how the product will win against competitors, alternatives, and the customer's option to do nothing.

**Try this.** For one target segment, state why a buyer should choose you over the leading alternative or doing nothing.

## Activity: Portfolio Management
id: portfolio-management
owner: Product Management

Evaluate the portfolio as a whole and allocate investment based on strategy, performance, opportunity, overlap, risk, and organizational capacity.

**Try this.** Review the portfolio and identify one product receiving investment that no longer earns its place.

## Activity: Prioritize Opportunities
id: prioritize-opportunities
owner: Product Management

Compare potential investments using agreed criteria, available evidence, expected outcomes, strategic alignment, and constraints.

**Try this.** Score a small set of opportunities using the same explicit criteria and evidence rather than stakeholder volume.

## Activity: Go/No-Go Decision to Develop
id: go-no-go-decision-to-develop
owner: Product Management

Determine whether to commit organizational resources to developing an initiative based on its expected value, evidence, risk, and strategic fit.

**Try this.** Before committing development resources, document what evidence, economics, and strategic conditions justify the investment.
