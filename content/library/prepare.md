<!-- The PREPARE activities, in the order an assessment pages through them. -->

## Activity: Coordinate Launch Readiness
id: coordinate-launch-readiness
owner: Product Marketing

Coordinate preparation across the functions needed to successfully introduce a product or release to its market.

**Try this.** List every function that must be ready for an upcoming launch and define what 'ready' means for each.

## Activity: Propose the Scope of the Launch
id: propose-the-scope-of-the-launch
owner: Product Marketing

Determine the appropriate scale, audience, objectives, and organizational effort for an upcoming launch.

**Try this.** Assign a launch tier to an upcoming release based on market impact and required organizational effort.

## Activity: Brief Go-to-Market Teams
id: brief-go-to-market-teams
owner: Product Marketing

Provide sales, marketing, customer success, support, and other teams with the product and market information needed for launch.

**Try this.** Ask Sales, Marketing, Success, and Support what they need to know or do differently before the next launch.

## Activity: Positioning
id: positioning
owner: Product Marketing

Define how the product addresses important market problems and how its value should be understood relative to alternatives.

**Try this.** Write the competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value, target customer, and market category for one product using Dunford's positioning approach.

## Activity: Messaging & Content
id: messaging-content
owner: Product Marketing

Translate positioning and product value into messages and content appropriate for target buyers, channels, and stages of the buying process.

**Try this.** Start with agreed positioning, then draft one message for a specific buyer and buying situation.

## Activity: Readiness Planning
id: readiness-planning
owner: Product Marketing

Assess and plan for organizational readiness across product, marketing, sales, support, operations, and customer success.

**Try this.** Run a cross-functional readiness review that identifies missing decisions, materials, training, and dependencies.

## Activity: Launch Planning
id: launch-planning
owner: Product Marketing

Define the activities, responsibilities, dependencies, timing, and measures required to execute a launch.

**Try this.** Create a launch plan that separates release readiness from market launch activities and ownership.

## Activity: Sales Enablement
id: sales-enablement
owner: Product Marketing

Equip customer-facing teams with the knowledge, tools, training, and materials needed to communicate and sell the product effectively.

**Try this.** Ask three sellers where deals stall and improve one enablement asset around the recurring obstacle.

## Activity: Buyer Experience
id: buyer-experience
owner: Product Marketing

Understand and improve the experience buyers have as they evaluate, purchase, and adopt the offering.

**Try this.** Walk through your buying process as a prospect and identify friction between first interest and successful adoption.

## Activity: Marketing Plan
id: marketing-plan
owner: Marketing

Define the programs, channels, audiences, investments, and activities used to create awareness, demand, and market engagement.

**Try this.** For one target market, identify audience, objective, message, channels, measures, and ownership before selecting tactics.

## Activity: Go/No-Go Decision to Launch
id: go-no-go-decision-to-launch
owner: Product Marketing

Determine whether the organization and market are sufficiently prepared to proceed with the planned launch.

**Try this.** Define the few conditions that must be true before launch and review them explicitly before proceeding.
