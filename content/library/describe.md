## Activity: Define & Communicate Product Direction
id: define-communicate-product-direction
owner: Product Management

Communicate product priorities, problems, intended outcomes, and direction so delivery teams understand what matters and why.

**Try this.** Explain a current product priority to a leader in terms of customer problem, business impact, evidence, and decision required.

## Activity: Problem Stories & Scenarios
id: problem-stories-scenarios
owner: Product Management

Describe market problems through stories, scenarios, workflows, and context that help teams understand the people and situations involved.

**Try this.** Rewrite one user story as a problem scenario that explains who is struggling, what they are trying to accomplish, and why it matters.

## Activity: Prioritize Potential Capabilities
id: prioritize-potential-capabilities
owner: Product Management

Evaluate potential capabilities based on customer problems, expected business value, evidence, strategic relevance, dependencies, and feasibility.

**Try this.** Compare proposed capabilities by the problems and outcomes they address rather than by requester seniority.

## Activity: Develop Product Requirements
id: develop-product-requirements
owner: Product Management

Describe the problems, capabilities, constraints, behaviors, and conditions needed to guide development of an approved solution.

**Try this.** Take an approved problem and document constraints, behaviors, and conditions without prematurely specifying unnecessary design details.

## Activity: Define Acceptance Criteria
id: define-acceptance-criteria
owner: Product Management

Establish observable conditions used to determine whether a capability addresses its intended requirements and behaves as expected. It's the definition of "done."

**Try this.** For one capability, write observable conditions that would demonstrate the intended behavior and outcome. Describe problems, not features.

## Activity: Communicate Status to Stakeholders
id: communicate-status-to-stakeholders
owner: Product Management

Keep relevant stakeholders informed about product direction, decisions, changes, risks, progress, and expected outcomes.

**Try this.** Replace a task-heavy status update with a briefing on outcomes, risks, evidence, decisions, and next steps.
