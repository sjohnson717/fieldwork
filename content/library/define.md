<!-- The DEFINE activities, in the order an assessment pages through them. -->

## Activity: Understand the Market
id: understand-the-market
owner: Product Management

Develop and maintain an understanding of customers, market needs, trends, technologies, and changes that may affect product opportunities and decisions.

**Try this.** Join three customer conversations and record problems, context, and language without asking for feature requests.

## Activity: Persona Definition
id: persona-definition
owner: Product Management

Identify meaningful patterns among buyers and users and represent their problems, contexts, behaviors, and roles in buying or using the product.

**Try this.** Review recent customers and prospects and identify patterns in problems, buying roles, context, and behavior before naming any persona.

## Activity: Problem Discovery
id: problem-discovery
owner: Product Management

Identify and investigate problems experienced by customers and the market through conversations, observation, feedback, and other sources of evidence.

**Try this.** Take five recent feature requests and rewrite each as the underlying problem the requester may be trying to solve.

## Activity: Synthesize Customer Evidence
id: synthesize-customer-evidence
owner: Product Management

Analyze customer conversations, research, support information, and field observations to identify recurring problems, themes, language, and patterns.

**Try this.** Combine notes from at least ten customer conversations and identify repeated problems, language, and contradictions; retain links to the source evidence.

## Activity: Competitive Research
id: competitive-research
owner: Product Marketing

Research competitors and alternative ways customers address the problem, including their positioning, capabilities, strengths, weaknesses, and market activity.

**Try this.** Compare how three competitors or alternatives address the same customer problem, including the option to do nothing.

## Activity: Manage Feature Requests
id: manage-feature-requests
owner: Product Management

Collect and interpret requests from customers and stakeholders to identify the underlying problems, needs, and potential opportunities they represent.

**Try this.** Choose ten requests and separate the requested solution from the underlying problem, evidence, and affected persona.

## Activity: Identify Evidence Gaps
id: identify-evidence-gaps
owner: Product Management

Distinguish what is known from what is assumed and identify important product or market questions that require additional evidence.

**Try this.** Take one proposed initiative and label each major claim as evidence, assumption, or unknown.

## Activity: Develop and Test Hypotheses
id: develop-and-test-hypotheses
owner: Product Management

Form possible explanations about customers, problems, markets, or opportunities and identify evidence that could support or challenge them.

**Try this.** Write three testable explanations for an observed customer problem and identify what evidence would change your mind.

## Activity: Product Brief
id: product-brief
owner: Product Management

Capture the initiative's opportunity, problem, evidence, assumptions, and rationale in a concise form for early-stage evaluation.

**Try this.** Summarize one opportunity on a single page: problem, persona, evidence, expected outcome, assumptions, and open questions.

## Activity: Solution Validation
id: solution-validation
owner: Product Management

Test proposed approaches and their underlying assumptions with representative customers or users before significant resources are committed.

**Try this.** Put a proposed solution in front of representative users and test the riskiest assumption before discussing implementation.

## Activity: Strategic Fit
id: strategic-fit
owner: Product Management

Assess whether the organization has the competencies, assets, resources, and capabilities needed to pursue an opportunity successfully.

**Try this.** Use ASPIRE to identify one capability that strengthens and one that constrains a current opportunity.

## Activity: Go/No-Go Decision to Pursue Initiative
id: go-no-go-decision-to-pursue-initiative
owner: Product Management

Determine whether an opportunity warrants further investment based on the problem, evidence, market opportunity, and strategic fit.

**Try this.** Review one early opportunity and explicitly document the evidence required before investing further.
