<!-- The DELIVER activities, in the order an assessment pages through them. -->

## Activity: Drive Outcomes & Improvement
id: drive-outcomes-improvement
owner: Product Management

Use market, customer, product, and business results to identify actions that improve product outcomes over time.

**Try this.** Choose one product outcome and identify the next decision you would make if the metric improves, stalls, or declines.

## Activity: Analyze Product Usage
id: analyze-product-usage
owner: Data / Analytics

Examine how customers and users actually use the product to identify adoption patterns, friction, abandonment, unexpected behavior, and opportunities for investigation.

**Try this.** Review usage for one important workflow and identify adoption, abandonment, friction, and unexpected behavior worth investigating.

## Activity: Monitor Performance & Metrics
id: monitor-performance-metrics
owner: Product Management

Track agreed product, customer, financial, and business measures to understand performance relative to expected outcomes.

**Try this.** Create a small product scorecard that connects customer behavior, product performance, and business results.

## Activity: Win/Loss Analysis
id: win-loss-analysis
owner: Product Marketing

Investigate why buyers selected the product, selected an alternative, delayed a decision, or chose not to act.

**Try this.** Interview three recent wins or losses and ask about trigger, alternatives, decision criteria, and why the final choice was made.

## Activity: Revenue Growth
id: revenue-growth
owner: Marketing

Develop and execute programs intended to attract prospects, create demand, acquire customers, and expand product revenue.

**Try this.** Identify one growth constraint and determine whether the limiting problem is awareness, acquisition, conversion, adoption, or expansion.

## Activity: Revenue Retention
id: revenue-retention
owner: Customer Success

Develop and execute programs intended to retain customers, encourage continued value realization, and support expansion or advocacy.

**Try this.** Review recent churn or renewal risk and separate product-value issues from service, relationship, pricing, or operational issues.

## Activity: Customer Success Stories
id: customer-success-stories
owner: Product Marketing

Document customer outcomes and experiences that demonstrate how the product creates value in actual use.

**Try this.** Interview one successful customer about the before-and-after business outcome rather than merely the features they use.

## Activity: Deliver Presentations & Demos
id: deliver-presentations-demos
owner: Sales / Sales Engineering

Present and demonstrate the product to prospects, customers, partners, or audiences in support of sales and market engagement.

**Try this.** Rework one demo around the buyer's problem and desired outcome rather than a tour of product features.

## Activity: Staff Promotional Events
id: staff-promotional-events
owner: Marketing

Represent and support the organization at conferences, trade shows, webinars, and other promotional activities.

**Try this.** Clarify the purpose of one event and the specific customer or market learning expected from participating.
