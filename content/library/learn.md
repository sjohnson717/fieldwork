<!-- The LEARN activities, in the order an assessment pages through them. -->

## Activity: Review Outcomes Against Assumptions
id: review-outcomes-against-assumptions
owner: Product Management

Compare actual customer and business results with the assumptions and expected outcomes that supported the original investment decision.

**Try this.** Choose an initiative launched several months ago and compare actual results with the assumptions used to justify it.

## Activity: Maintain Product Knowledge
id: maintain-product-knowledge
owner: Product Operations

Organize research, evidence, decisions, assumptions, market observations, results, and lessons so they can be found and reused in future product work.

**Try this.** Choose one place where research, decisions, evidence, and lessons can be retrieved later and move a recent initiative's material into it.

## Activity: Synthesize Product Learning
id: synthesize-product-learning
owner: Product Operations

Combine evidence from research, product usage, customer feedback, sales, support, experiments, and business results to identify meaningful patterns and implications.

**Try this.** Combine research, usage, sales, support, and outcome evidence for one product and write the three most important implications.

## Activity: Share Product Learning
id: share-product-learning
owner: Product Operations

Make relevant findings, evidence, decisions, and lessons accessible to teams that need them for future decisions and work.

**Try this.** Take one important recent learning and make it accessible to the teams whose future decisions should change because of it.
