Rate your own experience, skills, and interest in each activity. Experience is what you have done, skills is how well you do it, and interest is how much you want to do more of it — three different things, and the differences between them are the point.

The result is a development profile that belongs to you. It is not a performance review, and there are no right answers.

**For the consultant.** Run it alongside a Team Gap Analysis, or on its own. Each person rates their own experience, skills, and interest across the same activities. You get a development profile per person and, crossed against the team’s gaps, an answer to the question every client asks next: can the people we have do the work we just agreed matters? It also shows who wants the work, which is frequently not the person who has been doing it.
