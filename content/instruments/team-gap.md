Rate each product activity twice: how much it matters to your business, and how well it is done today. Then name who actually does it.

The distance between those two ratings is the finding, and it usually turns up somewhere nobody expected. You will only see the activities chosen for this assessment, not the whole list.

**For the consultant.** The engagement instrument. A team rates each activity on how much it matters and how well it is done today, and names who actually does it. You choose which activities to include, so the survey fits the client rather than the catalog. What comes back is a ranked set of gaps with names attached: work everyone agrees is critical that nobody owns, and work done meticulously that barely matters. That list is the agenda for the next six months.
